    Deal Ticket Reference¶

    This page lists every field, button and message in the Deal Ticket. For step-by-step instructions, see Open a New Position, Entry Orders and OCO Orders.

    Order types¶

    The four tabs at the top of the ticket. Ctrl+Tab moves between them.

    Tab What it does When it trades
    Market Buys or sells straight away at the price shown. Immediately.
    Limit Waits for a better price: buys lower, sells higher. When the market reaches your rate.
    Entry Waits for the market to move your way: buys higher, sells lower. When the market reaches your rate.
    OCO Places a Stop and a Limit together; the first to trade cancels the other. When either one is reached.

    Fields¶

    Field What it is Starts at Accepts
    Buy / Sell Which way you are trading. Alt+B and Alt+S also switch it. The side you clicked —
    Amount The trade size. Its label follows your size setting: Amount (Lots), Amount, or Amount, K. A size suited to your account, never below the instrument's minimum Up to 8 decimals; a trailing K is accepted
    Rate The order price. On the Market tab it follows the live price and cannot be edited. The current Bid or Ask Up to 6 decimals
    Distance in pips Limit and Entry only. How far from the market the order waits; it stays in step with Rate. Your default entry distance, never below your broker's minimum Up to 1 decimal
    Trader Range Market only. How many pips the price may move against you and still fill. 0 = exact price only. Your Default Trader Range setting Whole numbers
    Preset Stop Attach a stop loss that starts working when the order fills. Adds Rate, Distance in pips and Projected P/L fields. Off; when ticked, your default stop distance Tick box
    Trailing Distance Makes the preset stop follow the price. Appears when Preset Stop is ticked. Off Tick box
    Preset Limit Attach a take profit that starts working when the order fills. Off; when ticked, your default limit distance Tick box
    Comment Your own note, saved with the trade. Empty Up to 100 characters
    • − and +, or the Up and Down arrow keys, change the amount. The arrows beside the rate boxes change a price.
    • The slider covers the usual range of sizes; to trade more, type the size.
    • Preset Stop and Preset Limit are not available on the OCO tab.
    • The two large buttons at the bottom place the order and show the side and price, for example Buy 1.08432. Enter places the order on the side selected.

    Messages you may see¶

    While you fill in the ticket, an amber line with a ⚠ explains anything that is wrong, and the Buy and Sell buttons stay dimmed until it is fixed.

    Message What it means What to do
    Enter a rate The Rate box is empty or zero. Type a price.
    Rate must be below current ask A Buy Limit must be below the market. Lower the rate, or click Use this.
    Rate must be above current bid A Sell Limit must be above the market. Raise the rate.
    Rate must be at least 10 pips from market Your broker does not accept orders closer than this (the number varies). Move the rate further away.
    Nearest valid: 1.08432 The closest price your broker accepts. Click Use this to fill it in.
    OCO stop must be above ask The Stop rate is on the wrong side. Raise the Stop Rate.
    OCO limit must be below ask The Limit rate is on the wrong side. Lower the Limit Rate.
    OCO stop (or limit) must be at least 10 pips from market That rate is too close to the market. Move it further away.

    After you click Buy or Sell, the line shows Placing order… (or Placing OCO order… / Placing hedge order…). The ticket then closes, or your broker's reason for refusing the order appears in red. A Copy button copies that message, for example to send to support.

    On a small window

    The Deal Ticket has a fixed width. If your window is very narrow, part of it can be cut off; make the window wider. On a short window the ticket scrolls.
