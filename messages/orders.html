    Order and Trade Messages¶

    Message Kind Meaning
    Buy 1.00 EURUSD @ 1.08432 Success An order from the Buy/Sell strip above the chart, or from Living Market, was accepted at that price.
    Order placed — #161203 Success Accepted, with the broker's order reference.
    Order failed Error Rejected. The reason — from your broker, or from the application's own checks — is shown beneath.
    Position #98765 closed Success That position is now closed.
    Order cancelled Info A pending order was removed.
    Order Executed — Buy EURUSD Success An order was filled and is now an open position — either a market order, or a pending order whose price was reached. The size, price and trade number are shown beneath.
    Stop loss updated Info Your stop-loss was changed by dragging it on the chart.
    Take profit updated Info Your take-profit was changed by dragging it on the chart.
    Order rate updated Info A pending order's trigger price was changed by dragging it on the chart.
    Order modified Info An order, stop or limit was changed from a dialog.

    With Quick Trade switched on, an order placed by clicking a Bid or Ask cell in the watchlist shows a short note inside the watchlist instead, such as "Buy EURUSD placed". See A.2 for the full list.
