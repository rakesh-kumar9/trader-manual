    The Watchlist¶

    The watchlist on the left of the Trading tab shows live prices. From here you can find any instrument, show it on the chart, and trade it.

    Watch how: Read the watchlist (1:50)

    Find an instrument¶

    • Category tabs: click All, Forex, Crypto, Indices, Commodities, Stocks or ETF. A category only appears if your broker offers instruments of that kind.
    • Search: type in the search box. It matches the symbol and the instrument name, and always searches every instrument, even on your own watchlist tabs. Press Escape or click ✕ to clear it.
    • Click a row to show the instrument on the chart. Point at a row to see its full name.

    The number beside the search box shows how many instruments are listed.

    Read the prices¶

    Column What it shows
    SYMBOL The instrument. Always shown.
    BID The price you sell at, with ▲ or ▼ for the last move.
    ASK The price you buy at.
    SPRD The spread: the difference between Bid and Ask. Green when it narrows, red when it widens.
    HIGH / LOW / % CHG Today's highest Bid, lowest Ask, and the change in %. Hidden until you turn them on (right-click a heading → Customize columns…).

    A dash (—) means no price has arrived yet. Click SYMBOL, BID, ASK or SPRD to sort. See Columns and Sorting.

    Trade from the watchlist¶

    To… Do this
    Buy Click the Ask price. The Deal Ticket opens on Buy.
    Sell Click the Bid price. The Deal Ticket opens on Sell.
    Set a price alert Point at the row and click the △ that replaces the spread.
    Open a chart Click the row (or press Alt+N).
    Watch how: Quick order and hedge (0:41)

    With Quick Trade on, clicking a price trades at once without the ticket. See One-Click Trading.

    Make your own watchlist¶

    1. Click the + at the right end of the category row.
    2. Type a name and click Create. A second row of tabs appears with your watchlist, marked with a star. It starts empty.
    3. Go to All, find an instrument, right-click it and choose Add to Watchlist ▶ and your watchlist.
    Watch how: Make your own watchlist (0:54)

    Right-click one of your watchlist tabs to Rename…, ◀ Move Left, ▶ Move Right or Delete Watchlist (you are asked to confirm). To take an instrument out, right-click it on your watchlist's tab and choose Remove from Watchlist.

    Watch how: Rename, move or delete watchlist tabs (0:46)

    The right-click menu¶

    Right-click an instrument for every action:

    Watch how: Watchlist right-click menu (1:00)

    Menu item What it does
    Open Chart Show it on the chart (Alt+N).
    Price Alert Set an alert (Alt+P).
    Market Order Buy / Sell Open the Deal Ticket for a market order (Alt+B / Alt+S).
    Entry Stop / Limit Buy / Sell Open the Deal Ticket for an entry order. See Entry Orders.
    Entry OCO Buy / Sell Open the Deal Ticket for an OCO order. See OCO Orders.
    Add to Watchlist ▶ Add it to one of your watchlists.
    Remove from Watchlist Only on your own watchlist tabs.

    Every order item opens the Deal Ticket first, so you can check the order before sending it. Right-clicking a Bid or Ask price gives a shorter menu with only the Sell or Buy orders, headed by the instrument, side and price.

    Watch how: More from the watchlist (0:46)

    Keyboard¶

    Click in the list first.

    Keys What they do
    Up / Down Move through the list (wraps round). The chart does not change until you press Enter or click.
    Home / End First / last instrument.
    Enter Show the instrument on the chart.
    Alt+B / Alt+S Buy / Sell the highlighted instrument (Deal Ticket, or at once with Quick Trade on).
    Alt+N / Alt+P Chart / price alert.
    Alt+F Jump to the search box.
    Alt+Q Quick Trade on or off.
    Menu key Open the right-click menu.
    Escape (in the search box) Clear the search.
    Left / Right (on the tabs) Move to the next category or watchlist tab.
